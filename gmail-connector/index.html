David Gmail Connector

David Gmail Connector lets its owner search and read email from their authorised Gmail accounts in Codex. It includes received messages, sent messages, complete conversations, labels and attachments.

The connector runs locally on the owner's computer and uses Google's sign-in process. Its current configuration grants read access only. It cannot send, modify or delete email.

This tool is for the owner's use and is not offered as a public email service. This website describes the tool; it does not connect to or display any mailbox.

Read the privacy policy for details about access, storage and revocation.
