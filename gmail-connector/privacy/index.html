Privacy policy

This policy describes David Gmail Connector, a local tool operated by David Sapir for accessing his authorised Gmail accounts through Codex.

Information accessed

With the account owner's consent, the connector accesses the account email address and Gmail data, including message headers, bodies, sent messages, conversations, labels and attachments. It requests read access only.

Purpose and processing

The data is used to search, retrieve, summarise and analyse email at the owner's direction. Retrieved content is provided to Codex for processing by OpenAI. The applicable OpenAI service terms and the owner's account settings govern that processing and retention. Google data is not sold or used by this connector for advertising or to train general purpose AI models.

Storage and retention

Google authorisation tokens are stored in encrypted files on the owner's computer. The encryption key is stored on that same computer, so protection also depends on the security of the device. The connector retrieves messages on request and does not maintain a separate mailbox archive. Content returned to Codex may remain in chat history, logs or files according to the owner's use and service settings.

Sharing

Google supplies authorised mailbox data to the local connector. Content requested through Codex is shared with OpenAI to fulfil the owner's requests. The connector has no public mailbox interface. This public information website contains no mailbox content, tokens or account credentials.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account connections and remove the local account credentials. Revocation prevents future access; previously saved chat content or files must be removed separately using the relevant service controls.

Google API data

David Gmail Connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions or requests concerning this connector, contact info@sapir.group.
